- `cameras.*.by_path` / `by_id` -> helps identify the physical camera.
- `cameras.*.image` -> saved screenshot path under `tools/devices/images/` when capture succeeds.
- `capture_status` and `capture_detail` -> explain why a screenshot was saved, skipped, or failed.

Do not describe this as a grading tool, submission tool, or teacher dashboard.
